# Love Tester AI audience

**Who:** teens and young adults with a crush or a new relationship, and couples who want something fun to do together. They share results with friends.

**What they search for:** love calculator, love test, love tester, compatibility test, name compatibility, zodiac love match, and love letter ideas.

**What they care about**
- A quick, fun result they can screenshot and share.
- Feeling seen: playful insights about their crush or partner.
- Ideas for romantic moments: prompts, messages, love letters.

**Tone for this audience:** casual, lively and inclusive. The informal "you" is normal in every market for this category.
