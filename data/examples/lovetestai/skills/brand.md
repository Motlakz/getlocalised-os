# Love Tester AI brand

**Positioning:** a playful love calculator and compatibility app. Classic love tests, updated with AI for quizzes, couple prompts, love letters and romantic ideas. It is entertainment first.

**Voice:** playful, flirty and warm, with light humour. Short, lively sentences. Romantic without being cheesy.

**Always**
- Keep the name "Love Tester AI" as-is; never translate it.
- Present results as fun and for entertainment. Compatibility scores are a game, not a verdict.
- Use the everyday local words for love tests, crushes and couples.

**Never**
- Claim results are scientific, accurate or predictive of a relationship's future.
- Pressure, manipulation or "find out if they're cheating" angles.
- Explicit or adult content.
