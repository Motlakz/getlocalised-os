# Play Store rules for BellyClock

- **Title (30):** app name plus the core tool, e.g. "BellyClock: <fasting calculator in the local language>". No emoji, no ALL CAPS, no "free", "best" or ranking claims.
- **Short description (80):** one sentence with the main benefit and the most natural search phrase for the tool.
- **Full description (4000):** open with what it does in two lines, then short scannable sections or bullet points on features. Search phrases can appear naturally a few times; repeating the same keyword again and again is keyword stuffing and against policy.
- **Health & Fitness policy:** no claims to diagnose, treat or cure anything. Where medical advice would matter, say to consult a healthcare professional.
- Don't mention other apps or brands.
