# BellyClock audience

**Who:** adults already curious about or practising intermittent fasting, often beginners who want to get the timing right. Many also try longer water fasts and want to know how long they have been fasting.

**What they search for:** a free fasting calculator or timer, an intermittent fasting tracker, and specific plans like 16:8. They want a tool, not a lifestyle programme.

**What they care about**
- Knowing exactly when they can eat again.
- A simple view of the current fast and its phases.
- Not being sold a diet or made to feel bad.

**Tone for this audience:** reassuring and matter-of-fact. Explain phases plainly and never talk down to beginners.
