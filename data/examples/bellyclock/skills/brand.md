# BellyClock brand

**Positioning:** BellyClock is your fasting clock, a focused fasting calculator and timer. It is not a "wellness operating system" and not a diet app. It tells you when your fast started, where you are in it, and when it ends.

**Voice:** clear, calm and quietly encouraging, like a friend who knows the science but doesn't lecture. Short sentences. Practical over inspirational.

**Always**
- Lead with the tool: calculate, time and track your fast.
- Keep the name "BellyClock" as-is in every language; never translate it.
- Name fasting styles the way people in the market name them (e.g. 16:8, OMAD, water fasting), using the local spelling.

**Never**
- Promise weight loss amounts, medical outcomes, or "detox".
- Use shame, guilt, "burn fat fast" or before/after language.
- Use hype words ("revolutionary", "ultimate", "miracle").
