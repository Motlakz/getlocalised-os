# AuraSage audience

**Who:** adults who want a steadier inner voice but dislike generic positivity, endless feeds, streak pressure and complicated wellness routines.

**What they search for:** daily affirmations, positive affirmations, self-love or confidence affirmations, and calm or mindfulness apps for a short daily moment.

**What they care about**
- Words that feel believable to *them*, not borrowed slogans.
- A few minutes a day, then getting on with life.
- Privacy: their words and recordings stay theirs.

**Tone for this audience:** gentle and respectful. Never preachy, never clinical.
