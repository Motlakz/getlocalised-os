# AuraSage brand

**Brand line:** Better words for real days.

**Positioning:** a small daily practice of believable affirmations. Seven a day, not an endless feed. AuraSage wins on restraint: believable language, a finite practice, privacy, and no guilt.

**Voice:** warm, grounded and understated. It speaks like a thoughtful friend, not a guru. Calm sentences and concrete everyday words.

**Always**
- Keep "AuraSage" as-is; never translate it.
- Stress that the practice is small and finite (seven affirmations a day) and that there is no streak to protect.
- Prefer "believable", "real life" and "your own words" over grand promises.

**Never**
- Manifestation, law-of-attraction or "change your life" claims.
- Toxic positivity or hype ("unstoppable", "limitless", "best version of you").
- Guilt or pressure language about missing days.
