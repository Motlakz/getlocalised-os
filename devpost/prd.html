GetLocalised OS: Product Requirements

An English Play listing becomes a native French, Spanish, or German one, in a hosted Playground and runnable locally, for a solo developer with several apps.

status: approved

The journey

Success: in under a minute, with no key, a judge sees English become native German, sees why the wording was chosen, and sees a command reshape a field.

Screen by screen

From scope to PRD

Scope said “predefined category and search flow”
→
Local search phrases per market, captured once from Play suggestions, dated and visible
Scope said “basic quality checks”
→
Phrase usage per field, repetition counts, limits, and “why this term” notes, with no scores
Scope said “edit a skill, re-run”
→
A / command menu: 5 seeded commands work without a key, with previews before applying
Scope said “BYOK live mode”
→
An OpenAI or Gemini key calls this repo's own engine, is used once, and is never stored
Scope put the CLI in “Later”
→
A thin local run, which also generates the seeded data

Decisions and boundaries

DECIDED Product decisions
  • Your own apps as examples: AuraSage, BellyClock, LoveTest AI
  • fr-FR, es-ES, de-DE for all three
  • Keyword option (a): a transparent check, no scoring method
  • The scraper runs once at build time, not live
  • Five seeded commands, one field at a time, with previews
  • Keys go to this repo's engine only, never another service
  • Slate design and the original UI patterns, recoloured greyscale
  • The product never mentions another edition
  • Skills: a separate section in the / menu, plus a read-only Skills panel (bottom right) under Commands (top right)
  • Keys last for the browser session; OpenAI and Gemini; errors name the problem and keep the field
DEFERRED Not in the proof of concept
  • Screenshot localization
  • Live fetch by package ID or URL
  • Combining commands
  • Full CLI with .getlocalised/ config
  • Docs, Skills, CLI, Examples, Self-hosting pages
  • iOS metadata
NON-GOALS Will not do
  • Measured volume, keyword scoring, competitor or market intelligence
  • Store upload or Play Console connection
  • Calling any service other than the user's model provider
  • Storing keys, accounts, or saved projects
  • Mentioning another edition
